Questions

Good to know

Every car is different. Heavier swirls, scratches or pet hair take longer. You get a from-price straight away, and we confirm the final figure when we see the car, before we start. Nothing extra is ever added without asking you first.

1-step lifts light swirls and suits newer or well-kept cars. 2-stage cuts out most swirls and light scratches. 3-stage is for a near-flawless, show-car finish. Not sure? We'll check your paint and tell you honestly.

For polishing and ceramic, yes: 1 to 3 days in our studio under proper lighting. Washes and valets can be done at your home or work.

Up to 5 years with the right upkeep: a gentle wash every couple of weeks and a top-up every 6 months. We show you how at handover, or the Gloss Club takes care of it for you.

Card, cash or bank transfer. Polishing and ceramic jobs need a £50 deposit to hold your slot, and it comes off the final bill.
